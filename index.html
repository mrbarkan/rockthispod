🔒 Critical Music Sync Protocol

To prevent file system crashes, data corruption, or finder hangs during future music syncs on macOS:

  • Always turn the iPod's physical HOLD switch ON before plugging it into your Mac.
  • This forces the device to boot into its original Apple Disk Mode cache layer rather than using Rockbox USB drivers, ensuring high-speed data stability on flash adapters.
